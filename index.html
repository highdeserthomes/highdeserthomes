Who we are

Bringing customer service and craftsmanship back to Central Oregon

High Desert Homes was founded to bring customer service and quality craftsmanship back to Central Oregon. In an environment where profit has driven the market for so long, we wanted to bring our customers to the forefront of our company.

Your project is not just a job for us. We want to build your dream and let you know that your budget and satisfaction are our top priorities.

What makes us different

No job too big, and no job too small

From start to finish, we treat every job with the same level of professionalism and craftsmanship.

Eco-conscious

We use green building practices to build the best product while keeping our environmental impact small. We offer upcycled products on request, and love using reclaimed materials to make your project unique.

Cost-effective

We design your project with high-end features at a price that won't break the bank, and get the most out of your budget. We also offer discounts for military, first responders and educators.

Locally sourced

Giving back to the community is one of our greatest values. We only use local subcontractors and local suppliers for our projects.

FAQs

Common questions

What services does High Desert Homes offer?

We provide new construction, disaster remediation, remodels and renovations, and design services. Our in-house services include painting, siding, finish trim, bath and kitchen remodels, framing, drywall and concrete.

Who is High Desert Homes?

A Central Oregon builder that takes pride in giving you the best product with the highest level of craftsmanship, no matter the budget. Our mission isn't building houses. It's building inspiration and community while creating the best product at the best price.

How does the project development process work?

First, we plan and design your project together. Once we have a clear goal, we begin construction. We finish with meticulous attention to detail so your new space is ready to enjoy, and we stay available for continued support.

Is financing available?

Yes, financing is available. Contact us for more information.

What kind of discounts do you provide?

We offer discounts for military, first responders and educators.
